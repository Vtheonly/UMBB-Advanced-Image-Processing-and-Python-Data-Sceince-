# Section 8: Advanced - Transport Layer Protocols & Mechanics

This section studies transport-layer mechanics, focusing on logical ports, socket programming, and both connectionless (UDP) and connection-oriented (TCP) transport architectures.

##  Topic Index
- [[08.01 Transport Layer Role & Service Delivery Models]] — Study transport-layer segmentation, process multiplexing, flow control, congestion management, and TCP vs. UDP profiles.
- [[08.02 Logical Ports & Socket Programming Architecture]] — Review well-known ports, socket APIs, listening vs. connection sockets, and TCP 5-tuple connection tracking.
- [[08.03 User Datagram Protocol (UDP) Architecture]] — Analyze UDP's design philosophy, 8-byte fixed header format, and checksum verification limits.
- [[08.04 TCP Header Architecture & Control Flags]] — Decode the TCP header field-by-field, including the six control flags (URG, ACK, PSH, RST, SYN, FIN) and the window field.
- [[08.05 TCP Connection Management - Handshake, Sequence Arithmetic & Loss Recovery]] — Trace the three-way handshake, sequence/ACK arithmetic rules, and packet-loss recovery behavior observed in graded exam traces.
