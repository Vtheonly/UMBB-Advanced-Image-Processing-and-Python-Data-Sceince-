# Section 1: Foundations of Physical & Data Link Layers (Ethernet & Switching)

This section covers the fundamental layered models and link-layer concepts of computer networking, focusing on reference architectures, physical identifiers, frame decodes, cabling, and local switching infrastructure.

##  Topic Index
- [[01.01 MAC Address Classification & Identification]] — Learn about 48-bit MAC formatting, the LSB (I/G) rule, and how to classify unicast, multicast, and broadcast addresses.
- [[01.02 Ethernet Frame Structure & Header Decoding]] — Study Ethernet II frame fields (including Preamble, SFD, and FCS), EtherType identifiers, and step-by-step raw hex frame decoding.
- [[01.03 Layer 2 Network Infrastructure (Hubs vs. Switches)]] — Analyze differences between physical hubs and intelligent link-layer switches, transmission media, Spanning Tree Protocol (STP) link transitions, and the L2-switching vs. L3-routing boundary.
- [[01.04 OSI & TCP-IP Reference Models]] — Master the OSI and TCP/IP layered architectures, protocol data units, and the encapsulation/decapsulation sequence.
- [[01.05 Transmission Media & Cabling Constraints]] — Select straight-through vs. crossover cables from device classes (MDI/MDI-X), with the Auto-MDIX caveat and the serial-link clocking rule.
- [[01.06 Wireshark Hexadecimal Frame Parsing Walkthrough]] — Full byte-offset map plus two graded walkthroughs: a raw 48-byte hex capture decode and a Wireshark SMTP packet-detail analysis.
