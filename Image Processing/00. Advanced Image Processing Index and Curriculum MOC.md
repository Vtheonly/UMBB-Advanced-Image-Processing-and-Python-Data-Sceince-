# 00. Advanced Image Processing Index and Curriculum MOC

## 1. Track Overview
The Advanced Image Processing (*Traitement Avancé des Images* - TAI) curriculum covers the mathematical foundations, spatial mechanics, and algorithm design patterns required to process, enhance, and reconstruct digital images.

## 3. Theory Note Map
The laboratory sequence above is backed by the theory notes of the chapter; see [[00. Chapter Overview and Navigation]] for the complete table of contents:
- Foundations and discretization: [[01.1. Physical Image Formation, Radiometry, and Optical Sensing]] through [[01.11. The Classical Vision Processing Hierarchy - Low, Mid, and High-Level Tasks]].
- Degradation and restoration: [[02.1. Physical Noise Models (PSF, Gaussian, Impulse Salt-and-Pepper, Speckle)]] through [[02.8. Partial Differential Equations in Restoration (Perona-Malik Anisotropic Diffusion)]].
- Frequency domain: [[03.1. 2D Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT), FFT Shift, and Convolution Theorem]] and [[03.2. Frequency Filters (Low-Pass, High-Pass, and Band-Reject Notch Filtering)]].
- Morphology: [[04.1. Set-Theoretic Foundations, Binary Grids, and Structuring Elements]] and [[04.2. Compound Operators (Opening, Closing, Gradient, and Zhang-Suen Thinning)]].
- Contrast and Retinex: [[05.1. Dynamic Range Expansion and Noise-Tolerant Contrast Stretching]] and [[05.2. Advanced Contrast Models (Histogram Specification and Land's Retinex Theory)]].
- Clustering and deep restoration: [[06.1. Feature Space Clustering (Mean-Shift and 2SP-DBSCAN by UMBB iVision)]] and [[06.2. Deep Learning Denoising (Patch MLPs, Residual DnCNN, and Uformer)]].
- Segmentation: [[07.1. Formal Segmentation Axioms and Homogeneity Predicates]] through [[07.8. Segmentation Evaluation Benchmarks (Dice DSC, Jaccard IoU, Hausdorff Distance)]].
- Super-resolution: [[08.1. Continuous Image Reconstruction (Nearest, Bilinear, and Keys' Bicubic Spline)]] through [[08.4. Document Image Super-Resolution (Bi-ESRGAN Dual Path Architecture)]].
