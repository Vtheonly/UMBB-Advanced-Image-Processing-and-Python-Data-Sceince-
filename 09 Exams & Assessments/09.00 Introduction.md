## How to Use This Archive

1. **Attempt first, verify second.** Solve each exercise under exam conditions, then compare against the
   official graded solution inside the same file.
2. **Trace back to theory.** Every file links the theory notes covering its concepts; revisit them when a
   step of a solution is unclear.
3. **Cross-reference recurring patterns.** Subnetting (FLSM/VLSM), route aggregation, TCP sequence
   arithmetic, and Cisco CLI configuration recur across years — fluency in those four areas covers the
   majority of the graded marks.
